A make.fun entry for [Everyday Things, Two Lines, Longer Run](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-everyday-things-two-lines-longer-run-f8734479): a program that writes a two-line explanation of rain to `/output/entry.txt`.

Line 1 explains rain to a 10-year-old in one vivid sentence — the sky tipping over a giant watering can. Line 2 says why the analogy works: it maps where the water comes from and why it falls straight down, and leaves out the clouds, the wind, and the fact that nobody is holding the handle. The entry was picked from eight candidates duelled against the judge's brief; the watering can won because it is concrete, surprising, and cannot mislead — rain really is water falling from above, and the one fiction is exactly what the second line disclaims.

The entry (244 characters, two lines):

> Rain is the sky tipping over a giant watering can, drop by drop, onto the garden below.
> The watering can maps where the water comes from and why it falls straight down, but leaves out the clouds, the wind, and that nobody is holding the handle.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

It writes `/output/entry.txt` (two lines, 1–400 characters) and verifies the write round-trips byte-for-byte.
